## Events and business services

Hand over your whole event, or get practical support with IT, printing, tax and accounts.

### Event management

**Corporate events & launches**Product launches, AGMs, staff parties and client evenings, managed end to end.

**Conferences & workshops**Venue, speakers, registration, AV and delegate logistics for single or multi-day events.

**Weddings & private celebrations**Ruracio, weddings, birthdays and anniversaries, with day-of coordination so the family can enjoy it.

**Team building & retreats**Off-site programmes with transport, accommodation and facilitated activities.

### Consultancy

**IT infrastructure maintenance**Set-up, servicing and troubleshooting of computers, networks, printers and servers for offices and businesses.

**Career guidance**CV and cover letter reviews, interview preparation, and advice on courses and career paths.

**Printing & large format printing**Documents, business cards, flyers and brochures, plus banners, roll-up stands, posters and signage.

**KRA services**KRA PIN registration, iTax returns filing, tax compliance certificates and eTIMS support.

**Books of accounts preparation**Recording income and expenses, ledgers, bank reconciliations and regular financial statements.

**Audit & accounting services**Preparing your records and schedules ahead of an audit, plus ongoing accounting support.

How it works

## From first call to final invoice

1. **Brief**A short call to understand the occasion, guests, date and budget.
2. **Plan & quote**A written proposal with budget, timeline and what's included.
3. **Book vendors**Venue, catering, décor and AV confirmed, with every contract reviewed.
4. **Event day**On-site coordination against the run of show, so you can focus on your guests.
5. **Wrap-up**Vendor payments settled and a short report on spend and outcomes.
